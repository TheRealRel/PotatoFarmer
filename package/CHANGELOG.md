# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release, with **Gullinbursti's Trough**, a feeding trough: bronze and wood with boar-head ends (hammer, Furniture, 10 Wood and 2 Bronze at a workbench). Load it with food from your hotbar; hungry animals within 25 m (the server's Reach setting) walk over and eat from it one bite at a time. The bowl shows what's inside, the food spills out if the trough is broken, and it works in multiplayer and on dedicated servers.
- Pieces are textured like Valheim's own: the same pixel size and sharp pixels.
