# Changelog

## 1.3.2
- No more warning about Sleipnir's Post in the game log at startup.

## 1.3.1
- Settings in one section instead of five, each starting with its piece ("Trough capacity", "Post rope length"), so Configuration Manager is quicker to look through. Your settings, and the server's, move over on the first start.
- The cask keeps following Potato Tweaks' fermenter speed and output with Potato Tweaks 1.5.17, which renamed them.

## 1.3.0
- New: Auðhumla's Lick, a salt lick. Tamed animals within 10 m stay fed twice as long between meals (Fed time and Reach settings), and it works with mods that change hunger. Built at a forge from 10 Stone, 4 Flint and 2 Tin.
- New: Sleipnir's Post, a hitching post for 4 animals on 4 m ropes (Animals per post and Rope length settings). Tie a tamed animal that's following you; it fights only what comes within its rope's reach. Shift + E unties them all and they follow you. The rope's loop is fitted to each animal's own neck, modded animals included. Built at a forge from 4 Core Wood and 1 Bronze.
- The mod page shows four icons for each piece, drawn from its model, and every piece's hammer icon now shows it from the front, slightly from above.

## 1.2.0
- New: Heiðrún's Cask, a great mead cask that brews several mead bases at once, 4 by default (Batches setting), each like a fermenter of its own. Fine wood and black metal with a goat carved into each end, built at a forge from 30 Fine Wood, 6 Black Metal, 4 Tar and 20 Iron Nails. It follows the game's fermenter times and amounts, and PotatoTweaks' fermenter settings when that's installed. One kind of mead base at a time unless the server turns on Mix mead bases.
- The trough and the roost are now built at a forge instead of a workbench.
- The trough is 10% bigger, and its texture is evened out so its underside is no longer chunkier than the rest.
- The roost's texture is re-made more cleanly, without blotches on flat areas.
- A rewritten mod page with small piece icons.

## 1.1.3
- Gullinbursti's Trough holds 100 pieces of food (was 40), and a full trough shows more food. A server that already has a config keeps its own number: set Capacity to 100 there.
- Mixed foods are mashed together in the bowl instead of lying in groups, and the food sits down in the bowl rather than on top of it. A new trough screenshot on the mod page.

## 1.1.2
- Gullinkambi's Roost is 1.3 times bigger (about 2.5 m long), so it stands over your hens.
- Up to 12 eggs show in the cubbies, four to a cubby, huddled together in a clutch at a natural size.
- A new screenshot of the bigger roost on the mod page, and a link to the author's Discord for questions (bug reports stay on GitHub).

## 1.1.1
- The models, textures, icon and screenshots are now under CC BY-NC 4.0; the code stays MIT.

## 1.1.0
- New: **Gullinkambi's Roost**, a fine wood and black metal nesting box with rooster-head ends (hammer, Furniture, 10 Fine Wood and 2 Black Metal at a workbench). It gathers the eggs your hens lay nearby, so the flock keeps laying, and keeps each egg's star level. With a fire nearby it hatches its eggs, a chick every 5 minutes after the first, to keep the flock at 6 hens and chicks (Flock size setting); eggs already warming by a fire are left to hatch, E switches gathering off, and Shift + E takes the eggs. The eggs show in its three cubbies.
- Screenshots on the mod page, and a clearer introduction.

## 1.0.0
- First release, with **Gullinbursti's Trough**, a feeding trough: bronze and wood with boar-head ends (hammer, Furniture, 10 Wood and 2 Bronze at a workbench). Load it with food from your hotbar; hungry animals within 25 m (the server's Reach setting) walk over and eat from it one bite at a time. The bowl shows what's inside, the food spills out if the trough is broken, and it works in multiplayer and on dedicated servers.
- Pieces are textured like Valheim's own: the same pixel size and sharp pixels.
